The daily run

Done before your coffee

01

Fetch

Every posting from the companies on your watchlist, on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, plus Remotive, Remote OK and HN’s Who’s Hiring.

02

Filter

Out go the wrong titles, the wrong seniority, fake-remote roles, pay under your floor, and anything you put off-limits.

03

Score

Your agent reads each survivor against your resume: a 0–100 fit, the reason, and the gaps.

04

Serve

Best first, in your own tracker. Anything you’ve already seen stays out of the line.

Take your number

Fifteen minutes at the counter

One command installs it and starts the setup. It reads your resume, asks about ten questions, drafts your company watchlist, tests the filters on today’s real postings, and schedules your weekday run.

  • Claude Code or Codex, signed in
  • Node.js 20 or newer
  • Your resume as a PDF, Word or text file

No npm? Download the repo as a zip from GitHub and open your agent in the folder.

  1. Paste this in a terminal.

    It makes a bread-line folder, installs the engine, and offers to open Claude Code or Codex on the setup.

    $ npm create bread-line@latest
  2. Answer the questions.

    Have your resume ready as a file. About ten questions, then it builds your filters, watchlist and tracker.

  3. That’s it.

    A fresh batch of scored jobs lands every weekday morning.

Your line, your data

It stays with you

Your resume, filters and job history live in a folder on your computer and in your own tracker page. There’s no Bread Line account and no server of ours.

It only reads

It reads public job boards. It never applies for you, never messages anyone, and never touches your inbox unless you turn on LinkedIn alerts.

It’s yours to tune

Role types, seniority, pay floor and off-limits industries are plain settings. Tell your agent “stop showing me agency roles” and it updates them.

What does it cost?

Bread Line is free. Scoring uses your own Claude or ChatGPT plan, about as much as a few minutes of chat each morning.

Which jobs does it cover?

Remote roles. It targets the US by default, and the setup can point it at another region. It works best for roles at tech companies that post on Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby. Hybrid and on-site roles aren’t supported yet.

What if my computer is off at 8?

With Claude Code, the run starts the next time the Claude app opens. With Codex, it waits for the next weekday. Either way you can say “run the job radar” any time, and a missed or repeated run never loses or duplicates jobs.

How do I get updates?

Run npm update @loafpickleww/bread-line in your Bread Line folder. Your profiles, filters and job history aren’t touched.

Can more than one person use one copy?

Yes. Each person gets a profile folder of their own, with their own filters, watchlist, tracker and schedule.
